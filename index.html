Trip Rules

We are all adults. Make good choices.

Rules

  1. Do not get in trouble.
    We will not bail you out. Call your mom or wife for that.
  2. Social Media.
    Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, etc. — many on this trip are police, fire, or have wives, kids, and jobs at home. Posting anything about the trip is off limits.

    If it’s a picture and everyone in it approves, have at it. Posting something about someone, or a picture of them without permission will not be permitted.
  3. Last rule: Do what you want, when you want.
    Don’t feel like golfing one day… don’t. Don’t want to go out to dinner… don’t. Do you.
  4. The “Colin McCann Rule.”
    If you’re late, you go off last. No complaints. No negotiations. No sympathy. You get who you get. Plan accordingly.
